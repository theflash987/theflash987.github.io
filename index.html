About

I am a PhD student at the School of Computer Science, University of Bristol, supervised by Prof. Nantheera Anantrasirichai and Prof. Alin Achim. I obtained a BSc degree with First Class Honours in Computer Science from the University of Bristol. Prior to starting my PhD, I completed one semester of the MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Edinburgh, where I attained Distinction-level grades. My current research focuses on video restoration and enhancement, with a particular interest in generative modeling approaches such as flow matching.
